## Verdict: NEGATIVE for the whole margin/row axis

1. Decode-native negatives confirm stage-3's partial structure does not
   survive the right suite: no fitted row separates on delivery surfaces.
2. row_recal on the full host rotates rows to suite-optimal separators,
   yet pack delivery STILL collapses → the rows' operating point was
   never the remaining problem.
3. The failure localizes to DELIVERY MECHANICS on this deep host: any
   wrap firing during natural decode at λ=0.5 turns output to comma
   salad (consistent with the stage-4 freeze-step0 damage).  The
   wrap/training geometry — adapters fitted against the 2-layer slice
   (12 layers upstream missing) — is the remaining named suspect.
