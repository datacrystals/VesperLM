Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in sugars. In
the light-dependent reactions, water molecules are split, releasing oxygen and
producing ATP and NADPH, which then power the Calvin cycle to fix carbon
dioxide into carbohydrate in the chloroplast stroma.

The CRISPR-Cas9 system acts as adaptive immunity in bacteria: fragments of
viral DNA are stored between repeated sequences and transcribed into guide
RNAs that direct the nuclease to matching invader DNA. As a laboratory tool,
the same mechanism enables targeted double-strand breaks and precise genome
editing.

Plate tectonics explains earthquakes, volcanoes, and mountain building as
consequences of rigid plates moving over the ductile asthenosphere. Where
plates diverge new ocean floor forms; where they converge one plate subducts,
recycling crust into the mantle and building volcanic arcs above.

Antibiotics that target bacterial ribosomes exploit structural differences
between prokaryotic 70S and eukaryotic 80S ribosomes. Resistance spreads
through plasmids carrying beta-lactamases and efflux pumps, which is why
stewardship programs that limit unnecessary prescriptions matter clinically.

Neurons communicate at synapses where an action potential triggers calcium
influx, vesicle fusion, and neurotransmitter release into the cleft. The
postsynaptic response depends on receptor types: ionotropic receptors act in
milliseconds, while metabotropic receptors trigger slower second-messenger
cascades.

Climate records from ice cores and ocean sediments show that atmospheric
carbon dioxide and global temperature have co-varied for hundreds of
thousands of years. The current rise in carbon dioxide, driven by fossil fuel
combustion, is occurring far faster than natural glacial-interglacial swings.

Vaccines present antigens so the adaptive immune system builds memory B and T
cells without causing disease. mRNA vaccines deliver the genetic instructions
for a viral surface protein, letting host cells briefly produce the antigen and
train the response; the mRNA is degraded within days.

Enzymes lower activation energy by stabilizing the transition state, not by
changing the overall free energy of the reaction. Michaelis-Menten kinetics
describes how the rate saturates as substrate concentration rises, with the
constant Km reporting the substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity.

The James Webb Space Telescope observes primarily in infrared, letting it see
through dust clouds and detect light from the earliest galaxies stretched by
cosmic expansion. Its segmented mirror unfolds in space and is aligned to a
fraction of a wavelength for diffraction-limited imaging.

CRISPR screens now knock out every gene in a cell population in parallel and
read out fitness effects by sequencing guide abundance before and after
selection. The approach maps genetic dependencies in cancer cell lines and
identifies synthetic-lethal pairs that single-gene studies would miss.
