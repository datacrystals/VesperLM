## ARM-1 passport incident (2026-10-21) — FAILED at restart cap, rescued

The arm did NOT reach its 1360-step token target. Timeline (all UTC):

- 10-19 17:02 launch; steps 0–75 healthy (val 9.8302@25, 8.0016@75).
- 10-20 attempts 5–8 each crashed at the **step-350 checkpoint WRITE** with
  `PytorchStreamWriter failed writing ... file write failed` — the box disk
  (/dev/sda2) was 100% full under co-tenant ingest floods (fail points
  8.7 MB / 1.72 GB written across attempts = filling in real time).
- Between attempts the driver's **pruner deleted every checkpoint except
  `step_75`**: its `sort -t_ -k2 -n` on full paths (packed with
  underscores) fell back to lexical ordering, which pins `step_75` as the
  permanent keep. Each attempt therefore resumed from step_75, rebuilt to
  350 (~3.8 h), died again at the save. ~15 h of GPU spend looped.
- 10-21 attempt 9: disk had room; progressed past 350 to **step 625**
  (steady 8k plateau confirmed: **9,056 tok/s, 240.0 M tokens at step
  600**). At 12:57 the disk refilled exactly during the step_625 write →
  **truncated 3.385 GB checkpoint.pt**. Attempts 10–12 then crashed on
  resume-load (`PytorchStreamReader ... no central directory`, ~60 s each)
  → `arm ... exceeded restart cap — skipping arm` at **13:02:55**.
- The corrupt step_625 was deleted manually; **newest valid passport state:
  step_75** (+ `step_best`, eval jsons through step 600 intact).
- **Rescue plan:** after arm 2 completes, `rm ~/e1_diet/passport_FAILED`,
  relaunch the (patched) driver → passport re-arms from step 75; ~550 steps
  (~19 h at plateau pace, ~29–33 h wall incl. ramp remainder) to rebuild;
